## Elevator Pitch

"Every industrial factory loses money when machines fail unexpectedly. TwinForge AI predicts failures before they happen — using AI, IoT sensors, and 3D Digital Twins — at a fraction of the cost of enterprise solutions. We turn reactive maintenance into predictive maintenance."

## Problem
- Machine downtime costs manufacturing $50B/year globally
- 51% of motor failures are bearings — all predictable
- Enterprise solutions cost $50K–$200K/year — unaffordable for SMEs

## Solution
- Plug-and-play IoT sensor kit (₹8,999 per machine)
- AI that predicts failures 5–10 days in advance
- Plain-language explanations engineers can act on
- 3D Digital Twin for visual health monitoring

## Traction
- Prototype: 3-phase induction motor (most common industrial machine)
- Architecture designed to scale to 100+ machine types
- 2026: College MVP → 2027: First paying customers

## Ask
- Seed round: ₹50 Lakhs
- Use: 3 engineer hires + cloud infrastructure + 5 pilot factory deployments
