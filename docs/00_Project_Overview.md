# TwinForge AI — Project Overview

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Status:** Active Development

---

## Summary

TwinForge AI is an Industry 4.0 platform that creates intelligent Digital Twins of industrial machines and predicts failures before they happen using Artificial Intelligence.

## Problem Statement

Industrial machines fail unexpectedly. This costs factories millions in:
- Unplanned downtime
- Emergency repair costs
- Lost production
- Safety incidents

Most small and medium factories cannot afford enterprise solutions like Siemens MindSphere ($100K+/year).

## Solution

TwinForge AI provides:
1. Real-time sensor monitoring via ESP32 IoT devices
2. AI-powered failure prediction (health score, RUL, failure type)
3. 3D Digital Twin visualization
4. Natural language Factory Assistant
5. Explainable AI — never a black box

## Target Market

- Small & Medium Manufacturing Industries (SMEs)
- Plant Maintenance Engineers
- Production Managers
- Factory Automation Consultants
