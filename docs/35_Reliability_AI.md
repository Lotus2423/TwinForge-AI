# Reliability AI

**Status:** Planned — Phase 2+

## Purpose

AI agent focused on overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) and reliability engineering calculations. Tracks mean time between failures (MTBF), mean time to repair (MTTR), and availability metrics.

## Key Metrics

- OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality
- MTBF = Total uptime / Number of failures
- MTTR = Total repair time / Number of repairs
- Failure Rate = 1 / MTBF
