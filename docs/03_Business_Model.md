# Business Model

## Revenue Strategy

### MVP Phase (2026)
- Free for pilot partners
- Data collection and model improvement
- Case study generation

### Startup Phase (2027)

**SaaS Subscription (Primary)**

| Tier | Price/month | Machines | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹4,999 | Up to 5 | Monitoring + Basic AI |
| Professional | ₹14,999 | Up to 25 | Full AI + Digital Twin |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | All features + SLA |

**Hardware Kit (Secondary)**
- TwinForge Sensor Kit: ₹8,999 per machine
- Includes: ESP32, sensors, mounting hardware, 1-year warranty

**Implementation Services**
- Setup & onboarding: ₹25,000 one-time
- Custom AI model training: ₹50,000+

## Unit Economics (Target 2027)

- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): ₹15,000
- LTV (Lifetime Value): ₹1,80,000+ (3 years Professional)
- LTV:CAC ratio: 12:1 (target)
- Gross margin: ~75% (SaaS)

## Total Addressable Market

- India SME manufacturing: 63 million units
- Target (Phase 1): Mechanical + electrical manufacturing
- 1% capture at ₹15K/year = ₹9,450 Cr market

## Go-to-Market

1. Direct sales to local factories (pilot phase)
2. Partnership with motor/pump OEMs
3. Channel partners: Industrial automation consultants
4. Content marketing: Failure case studies
5. Government: Make in India / Industry 4.0 schemes
